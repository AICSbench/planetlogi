## 1. Multi-Curriculum Architecture (6 Tracks)

PlanetLogi organizes skills using a **Canonical Skill Graph** underneath **6 selectable Curriculum Tracks**. A single underlying mathematical skill (e.g., *2-digit addition with regrouping*) can be mapped into multiple curricula so a student's mastery transfers intelligently while respecting each syllabus's exact grade placement and terminology.

| # | Track Code | Display Name (EN) | Display Name (ID) | Grade Scope | Purpose |

| 2 | `SINGAPORE_MOE` | Singapore Math MOE | Matematika Singapura (MOE) | Primary 1–6 | Mastery-driven concrete-pictorial-abstract (CPA) & bar-model curriculum (~250+ skills per grade across Core, Word Problems, Challenging) |

## 2. Grades 1–6 Topic Scope Matrix

### Grade 1 (Primary 1 / Fase A — *Terra Pebble*)
- **Number & Operations**: Whole numbers 0–100 (counting, numerals & words, place value tens/ones, comparing & ordering, number bonds), Addition & Subtraction within 20 & 100 (with/without regrouping), Introduction to Multiplication (equal groups, repeated addition) & Division (sharing/grouping).
- **Measurement & Geometry**: Length (non-standard & cm), Mass, Time (o'clock & half-past), Money (IDR Rupiah & USD/SGD coins/notes per locale), 2D Shapes & Patterns.
- **Data**: Picture Graphs (Pictograms).
- **Word Problems & Challenging**: 1-step addition/subtraction stories, visual number patterns.

### Grade 2 (Primary 2 / Fase A — *Planet Tropica*)
- **Number & Operations**: Whole numbers up to 1,000, Addition & Subtraction within 1,000 (vertical regrouping), Multiplication Tables of 2, 3, 4, 5, 10 & related Division facts, Mental Calculation, Introduction to Fractions (unit & like fractions, comparing, adding/subtracting like fractions within 1 whole).
- **Measurement & Geometry**: Length (m, cm), Mass (kg, g), Volume (litres), Time (to 5 minutes, AM/PM), Money (making change), 2D/3D Shapes (flat & curved surfaces, quarter/half turns).
- **Data**: Picture Graphs with Scales.
- **Word Problems**: 1-step and 2-step bar-model word problems.

### Grade 3 (Primary 3 / Fase B — *Planet Aquaris*)
- **Number & Operations**: Numbers up to 10,000, Addition & Subtraction within 10,000, Multiplication Tables of 6, 7, 8, 9, Multiplying 3-digit by 1-digit, Division with Remainders, Equivalent Fractions, Comparing Unlike Fractions, Adding/Subtracting Related Fractions.
- **Measurement & Geometry**: Length (km, m, cm), Mass (kg, g), Volume (l, ml), Time (seconds, duration, 24-hour intro), Area & Perimeter of squares/rectangles, Angles (right, acute, obtuse), Perpendicular & Parallel Lines.
- **Data**: Bar Graphs (vertical & horizontal).

### Grade 4 (Primary 4 / Fase B — *Planet Crystalia*)
- **Number & Operations**: Numbers up to 100,000, Factors & Multiples (LCM/GCF intro / KPK & FPB), Multi-digit Multiplication (up to 4×1 and 2×2) & Division, Mixed Numbers & Improper Fractions, Fraction of a Set, Decimals (tenths, hundredths, thousandths, rounding, 4 operations with decimals).
- **Measurement & Geometry**: Time (24-hour clock & complex duration), Area & Perimeter of Composite Figures, Measuring Angles with Protractor, Squares/Rectangles properties, Line Symmetry.
- **Data**: Tables & Line Graphs.

### Grade 5 (Primary 5 / Fase C — *Planet Lumina*)
- **Number & Operations**: Numbers up to 10,000,000, Order of Operations (BODMAS/PEMDAS), Multiplying & Dividing Fractions, Multiplying/Dividing Decimals by 10/100/1000 & whole numbers, **Percentage** (part of whole, discounts, GST/PPN tax, interest), **Ratio** (equivalent ratios, 3-quantity ratios), **Rate**.
- **Measurement & Geometry**: Area of Triangles, Volume of Cubes & Cuboids (`cm³`, `m³`, litres), Angles on a Straight Line, Vertically Opposite Angles, Angles at a Point, Properties of Triangles (isosceles, equilateral, right-angled) & Four-Sided Figures (parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium).
- **Data**: Average (Mean).

### Grade 6 (Primary 6 / Fase C — *Planet Solaris*)
- **Number & Operations**: **Algebra** (algebraic expressions, simplifying, evaluating, simple linear equations), Dividing Fractions by Fractions, Advanced Ratio & Percentage Change, **Speed** (Distance–Speed–Time, average speed, catching-up/meeting problems).
- **Measurement & Geometry**: **Circles** (radius, diameter, circumference, area, semicircles/quadrants, composite circular figures), Volume of Cubes/Cuboids with water-level & flow-rate problems, Finding Unknown Angles in Composite Geometric Figures, Nets of 3D Solids.
- **Data**: Pie Charts & Multi-Source Data Interpretation.
- **Exam Readiness**: Full Primary 6 / Ujian Sekolah / PSLE-style synthesis & HOTS mastery.

## 4. Mastery, Proficiency & High-Score Algorithms

### 4.1 Skill Proficiency % Calculation
To reflect current mastery accurately without permanently punishing early learning mistakes:
- **Sample Window**: Computed over the student's **most recent 20 valid attempts** on a canonical skill (across Daily Orbit, Galaxy Training, and Assignments; Space Academy *Do & Learn* retries count only the first check per question to prevent inflation).
- **Proficiency Status Tiers**:
  - **Incomplete**: `< 10` total attempts on this skill (displayed as `X / 10` progress toward unlocked proficiency).
  - **Beginning**: `0% – 59%` accuracy (`>= 10` attempts) — Amber badge.
  - **Competent**: `60% – 79%` accuracy (`>= 10` attempts) — Blue badge.
  - **Mastery**: `80% – 100%` accuracy (`>= 10` attempts) — Emerald badge.
- **National Avg %**: Nightly background job computes median/mean proficiency per skill across active students in the same grade & country/global cohort (cached in Redis; falls back to curated pedagogical benchmark when cohort `< 50` students).

### 4.2 High Score & 3-Star Rating Calculation
Tracked per skill based on the student's **best 10-question Galaxy Training session** (`0` to `10` correct):
- `9 – 10 / 10` → **3 Stars (`★★★`)**
- `6 – 8 / 10` → **2 Stars (`★★☆`)**
- `4 – 5 / 10` → **1 Star (`★☆☆`)**
- `1 – 3 / 10` → **0 Stars (`☆☆☆`)** — recorded in High Score Report under the `1–3` improvement tier.

### 4.3 Daily Orbit Adaptive Selection Algorithm
When generating a student's 10-question Daily Orbit set:
1. Pull eligible skills from the student's **active Grade** and **enabled Curriculum filters** (default: HOTS + Singapore MOE or Kurikulum Merdeka based on locale).
2. Weight selection: **40%** *Beginning/Weak* skills, **30%** *Unattempted/Incomplete* skills, **20%** *Competent* reinforcement, **10%** *Mastery* spaced-repetition review.
3. Map question difficulty (`1–5` flames) across the 10-node path and student's selected Tier (**Elementary** = diff 1–3, **Intermediate** = diff 2–4, **Advanced** = diff 3–5, **Master** = diff 4–5 + HOTS).
4. Guarantee zero duplicate question IDs or `(templateId, seed)` pairs seen by the student in the last 30 days.

## 5. Automatic Annual Grade Promotion

- Schools and individual accounts carry an `academicRolloverDate` (default: **July 1** for Indonesian/Southeast Asian schools, configurable to **September 1** or **January 1** per school/country).
- Displayed transparently on the student's Profile screen (e.g., *"Scheduled promotion to Grade 2 on 1 Jul 2027"*).
- Scheduled idempotent cron job promotes `Grade 1 → Grade 2 → ... → Grade 6` (and marks `Grade 6 → Graduated/Archive` while retaining read/review access), while preserving all historical SP, AC, XP, Badges, Stories, and Proficiency reports across past grades.
- Parents or School Admins can manually override a student's grade at any time in Profile Settings / Teacher Portal.
